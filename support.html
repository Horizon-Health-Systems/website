Online Support

Welcome to Horizon Health Systems Online Support. We provide reliable technical assistance and professional guidance to help you use our healthcare management solutions effectively. Our support team is ready to assist you with technical issues, system configuration, troubleshooting, and remote assistance.

How Can We Help You?

Follow our support instructions or contact our team for professional technical assistance.

How to Get Remote Support

Follow these simple steps to receive remote technical assistance from our support team.

  1. Contact Horizon Health Systems support.
  2. Explain your technical problem to our team.
  3. Open the approved remote support application on your computer.
  4. Enter the support code provided by our authorized technician.
  5. Confirm the connection and allow assistance only when you trust the technician.

Security Notice: Never share your passwords or confidential patient information.
